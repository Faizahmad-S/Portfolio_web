01 — About

From fragmented data to decisions

I work on end-to-end analytics projects: taking fragmented, real-world datasets and turning them into structured, decision-ready insight. Across my portfolio I've built SQL analytics pipelines in PostgreSQL, segmented customers into value tiers with window functions and CTEs, tracked Year-over-Year product performance, and designed interactive Power BI and Excel dashboards used to explore markets, sales, and hiring trends.

What I care about most is the last step — making the insight clear enough that someone can act on it without needing to understand the pipeline behind it.

02 — How I Work

Every project follows the same discipline

step_1

Clean

Standardise raw data — duplicates, types, missing values — into a reliable analytical table.

step_2

Explore

Profile the data, define business boundaries, and establish core KPIs worth tracking.

step_3

Analyse

Answer the business questions — trends, segmentation, YoY growth — with SQL and DAX.

step_4

Report

Deliver interactive dashboards and views that stakeholders can filter and act on.
