Bike Sales Analysis
  • Projects
  • Project Details

Purpose

Analyze customer and demographic data to identify the main factors influencing bike purchase decisions.

The project translates raw customer records into actionable insights that can support marketing and sales strategy.

Tools

Excel | Pivot Tables | Pivot Charts | Slicers | Dashboard Design

Summary

Built an interactive Excel dashboard to analyze how income, age, commute distance, occupation, and region affect bike purchases.

The dashboard enables dynamic filtering and segmentation, helping stakeholders explore customer behavior and identify high-potential target groups.

What I Did

  • Cleaned 1,026 raw customer records into 1,000 analysis-ready rows by removing 26 duplicates and converting coded values (M/F, M/S) and raw ages into readable labels and age bands in Excel.
  • Identified the strongest buyer segment, the 30–39 age group, with a 61.1% purchase rate and 40.1% of all buyers, by building pivot tables of purchase rate by age band.
  • Showed how strongly commute distance shapes demand: 0–1 mile commuters make up 41.6% of buyers, while the purchase rate falls to 29.7% for commutes over 10 miles, found by analysing purchases across commute bands.
  • Showed that buyers earn more, with an average income of 57,963 against 54,875 for non-buyers (5.6% higher) for both men and women, by comparing average income by gender and purchase status.
  • Let stakeholders explore customer segments themselves across marital status, region, occupation and gender by building an interactive Excel dashboard with slicers.

Key Insights

  • Income is a strong purchase driver, with bike buyers showing noticeably higher average income levels.
  • The 30–39 age group represents the strongest buying segment, contributing the highest share of purchases.
  • Customers with a 0–1 mile commute make up the largest share of buyers (41.6%), while purchase rates fall to 29.7% for commutes over 10 miles.
  • The Married + North America + Professional segment shows the highest income levels, but higher income alone does not always guarantee higher purchase rates.

Outcome

Created an interactive Excel dashboard that helps identify target customer segments and supports more focused marketing decisions based on income, age, commute behavior, and demographic patterns.
